I’m a self-taught developer who learns best by building something, running into a problem, and working out why it happened. This is a place to document the systems underneath the software: virtual machines, routing, firewalls, and the connections between them.

## What I’m working with

My hands-on experience includes **Proxmox VE**, experiments with a virtualized **OPNsense** router, **Tailscale**, and troubleshooting LAN access. I also own used enterprise **10GbE adapters** that I want to put to better use.

I also have roughly 900 feet of UTP Cat6a cable left from a 1,000-foot roll and the tools to terminate it, so there is room for hands-on cabling practice alongside the virtual networking work.

The [lab overview](/lab/) separates established experience from the details I still need to document. Exact hardware models and the current topology are waiting to be confirmed.
